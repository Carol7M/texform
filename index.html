TexForm · About Us

Who we are

TexForm Structures Ltd is a subsidiary business unit of Kenya Tents Ltd, formed in 2014 specifically to design and manufacture tension membrane structures. We also undertake general structural steel fabrication.

We are one of the leading manufacturers of tents, fabric structures and related equipment in East and Central Africa. We operate from two sites, one for sales and administration and the other for production, with a staff complement of 20.

Our Mission
“To provide customers with fabric structures to world class quality standards through the use of advanced technology, striving to better understand our customers and improve our practices to meet their needs.”

TexForm · Services

Our core competencies

From the first conceptual sketch to the final tensioned membrane, four disciplines carry every structure through to completion.

01 · Design

Design

A substantial part of our activities are geared towards conceptual design. Our design and engineering team works closely with architects, engineers, planners, contractors and owners to identify the aesthetic and practical requirements of a project during the conceptual design phase. From this effort we advise clients on:

  • Suitability of various fabric forms for their project
  • Material considerations: PVC, PTFE, ETFE
  • Preliminary loadings
  • First cost estimates
  • Project management
  • Communication drawings: 2D, 3D and renders
02 · Engineer

Engineer

Our engineers are specialists in lightweight structures who work with sophisticated software tools to produce bold structural systems in co-ordination with the design team. Software packages are used for simulation, analysis and optimization. In many cases we use parametric tools to collapse the solution space and arrive at the most structurally efficient and aesthetic configurations.

03 · Fabricate

Fabricate

We have over 45 years of experience in fabric and steel fabrication. Our 9,000 sq ft cutting and plotting room houses several types of cutting and welding machines, with a dedicated staff of 14 people.

04 · Build

Build

We have a dedicated team of riggers, installers, welders and site foremen, armed with the necessary equipment to realise even the most complex tasks.

TexForm · FAQs

Common questions

Here is a list of the questions we get asked most often.

Our structures are designed to be quick and easy to install by our team of trained personnel. For temporary structures we can show you how to erect and dismantle your canopy and provide written instructions to make it as simple as possible. The average structure takes less than 6 weeks to manufacture and install.

We offer a choice of PVC coated polyester and silicone coated glass cloth for all of our structures. Both are watertight and high quality but offer different translucency, colour options and durability.

Tensile fabric is not difficult to maintain at all. We recommend an annual inspection and maintenance for all our structures just to make sure everything is working properly and looks as good as new. Our prices allow for the complete structure above ground. The most commonly overlooked cost is the design and construction of foundations. We will supply engineering analysis information to your ground works contractor, enabling them to supply the foundations.

Most of our structures channel water to the perimeter of the canopy and then let it run off naturally. For some structures we can install a diverter or gutter which redirects the water to the ground and out of the way.

Not all types of structure require planning permission, but we would advise you check with your local planning authorities before placing an order. We can provide drawings and further information to support your planning application if necessary.

For smaller sales that require lighter grade fabrics there is a range of colour options available. For wider span structures such as airports, stadia roofs and commercial spaces that require heavier grade fabrics, the only colour option available is white. Special colours can be made by the manufacturer for projects of 5,000m² and above.
